Last updated: August 26, 2026 — applies to the Pak-a-boo Chrome extension, all versions.

Privacy Policy

Short version: Pak-a-boo collects nothing. There are no accounts, no backend server, and no analytics of any kind. Everything the extension needs — your break schedule, today's stats, your language and focus settings — is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage, and never transmitted anywhere.

No data collection No accounts No servers No analytics No remote code

What's stored, and where

All of the following stays on your device, inside Chrome's local extension storage, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the extension:

  • Your break rhythm progress (cycle position, today's completed-break count)
  • Your preferences (language, focus mode, on/off state)

None of this is synced to any external service, sent over the network, or accessible to anyone but you.

Why each permission is needed

  • alarms — schedules break reminders reliably, since the extension's background process can be suspended by Chrome at any time.
  • storage — saves your schedule and preferences locally, as described above.
  • idle — detects when you've stepped away from your computer, so a long absence is treated as a break already taken rather than an ignored reminder.
  • notifications — shows a native system notification when a break is due, as a fallback for when the in-page reminder isn't visible.
  • scripting — injects the reminder overlay into tabs that were already open before the extension was installed or updated.
  • Host permissions (all sites) — the reminder overlay needs to appear on whatever page you're viewing when a break comes due, since reminders are timing-based and not tied to any specific site. The extension does not read, modify, or transmit the content of any page — it only displays its own overlay UI.

Third parties

Pak-a-boo does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only external link in the extension is an optional, entirely voluntary "buy me a cocoa" donation link to a third-party payment page (Ko-fi), which you are never required to visit or use.

Children's privacy

Pak-a-boo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age — because it does not collect information at all.
